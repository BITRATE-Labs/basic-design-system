# 4. State and status colour

Compare these in **Compare** and **Contrast in every brand**. The ratios printed under each column are measured from the rendered colours.

## Loominist brand tint as the hover and active wash

Hover, selected and current states are painted with the brand colour at a low share over the surface instead of a neutral grey: 5% for hover, 3% for a selected row and 7% plus brand-colour text for the current nav link. It comes from Loominist's `brand.css`, `application.css` and `setup.css`, where the values are hex-alpha literals on its copper; here they are `color-mix(in srgb, var(--primary) N%, surface)`, which paints the same colour and follows each brand's primary.

Adopting it is small: set `--accent` per brand from that mix, and add slot rules for ghost and outline buttons and table rows, because stock hovers those on `--muted`; ghost and outline buttons also need a `data-variant` attribute, which the stock button does not write. The risk is visibility: Loominist has no light theme, and at these shares the hover wash measures 1.06 to 1.11:1 against a light page, about what stock's grey gives, so the light percentages would need retuning; selection still needs a non-colour cue. The current nav link's primary text on the 7% wash also measures 4.00:1 in chronote light, 4.01:1 in vrdex light and 4.43:1 in loominist light, below 4.5:1, where stock's foreground text is 15 to 20:1.

## Loominist brand tint, adjusted for contrast

The same wash with the current nav link's text in `--foreground` instead of the primary, so the 7% wash alone marks the current page. This is not Loominist's rule. It measures 12.8:1 or more in every brand and theme, at the cost of the brand-coloured current link.

## drasil status triads and the left-rule callout

Each status (success, warning, info, destructive) gets a text colour, a soft wash and a line, retuned for dark rather than inverted, and a callout can be a 3px inline-start rule with 10px padding and no box. It comes from drasil's `globals.css` (the triad tokens, `.status` and `.cleanup-coverage`); the chip's uppercase 800-weight shape is left out.

Adopting it means `-soft` and `-line` roles (and a text role) for four statuses in every brand and theme, plus Alert styling on `[data-slot=alert][data-tone]`; the derived column shows one way to build them from the existing status roles, with the text mixed 60% role to 40% foreground so it keeps 4.5:1 in all five brands. The risks are that the callout's rule measures 1.3 to 2.5:1 against a light page in every brand and below 3:1 in some dark ones (drasil's own light lines are similar), and that forced colours turn every line the same colour, so the title must name the status.
