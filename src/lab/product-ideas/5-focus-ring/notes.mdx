# 5. Focus ring

Compare these in **Compare**; the other stories focus one real element by keyboard. To see forced colours, open DevTools, Rendering, and emulate `forced-colors: active`.

## Loominist solid brand outline

Focus is a solid 2px outline in the brand colour, replacing the stock 3px ring at 50% of `--ring`: 5px out on buttons and links, as Loominist's brand-wide `a, button, summary` rule sets it, and 3px out on inputs, textareas and checkboxes, as its setup form controls do (its 4px for cards and header links has no counterpart among these subjects). Inside menus, scroll areas, tab bars and the sliding nav it is drawn 2px inside the item so the container does not clip it, taken from Chronote's picker and Perkcord's menu items; a focused menu item also takes Perkcord's 10% primary wash instead of the stock `--accent` highlight, because in brands where `--accent` is the primary (loominist) the outline vanished into it (1.00:1). The colour is `--primary` because Loominist's ring is its brand colour, while several brands here have a grey or translucent `--ring`.

Adopting it is one block of unlayered theme CSS on the components' `data-slot` attributes, which also clears the stock glow; every visual baseline with a focused control changes. In forced colours the stock ring is a box-shadow and disappears, while the outline stays and takes the system highlight; the known risks are an outline at 5px colliding with neighbours in tight button groups, the menu ring measuring 3.25:1 on the wash in chronote dark, close to the 3:1 floor, and the stock 150ms transition growing the outline in, which the reduced-motion path removes.
