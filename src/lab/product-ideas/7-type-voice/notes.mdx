# 7. Type voice

## Stock sizes

Headings and text use Tailwind size utilities chosen per component: `text-2xl font-semibold` for a page title, `text-base font-medium` for a card title, `text-sm` for nearly everything else. Nothing names the role a piece of text plays, so products drift between `text-sm` and arbitrary sizes.

## Role-named type scale (Perkcord)

Ten roles (hero, display, title, title-compact, section, heading, body, reading, meta, caption), each bundling size, line-height and, for headings, weight, with -0.02em tracking on the page title and -0.01em on section headings; it comes from Perkcord's `@theme static` block and base heading styles in `globals.css`. Adopting it is small: role tokens in the shared scales, overridable per brand and emitted as Tailwind `@theme` text tokens, while stock components keep `text-sm` and products and Examples use the roles. The risk is a second vocabulary beside the stock sizes, so the roles only hold if the Examples and product pages use them consistently.

## Eyebrow and display heading (Loominist)

An 11px, weight 600, 0.15em-tracked uppercase eyebrow in the brand's primary colour (mixed with a quarter of the foreground to clear 4.5:1 at 11px) above a display-face heading at 52px/1.15, weight 500, -0.035em, with section titles and legends at 25px/1.25, -0.015em and balanced wrapping; it comes from Loominist's `shaping.css`, `setup.css` and `home.css`, where the eyebrow is 10px. Adopting it is small: an eyebrow role and display tracking tokens beside the role scale, with the face taken from each brand's `--font-display`. The risk is the template look of an eyebrow over every heading, and uppercase tracking hurts readability beyond one to three words.
