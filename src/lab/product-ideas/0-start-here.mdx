# Product ideas

Eight choices, each comparing stock shadcn with styling and motion taken from the products. Every group has a Compare story and a Notes page. Switch brand and light or dark from the toolbar.

- [1. Surfaces](?path=/story/lab-product-ideas-1-surfaces--compare): stock card, menu and dialog surfaces against Perkcord's lit edge without blur and its lit-edge glass.
- [2. Motion and timing](?path=/story/lab-product-ideas-2-motion-and-timing--compare): stock popup timings against Perkcord's motion tokens with a reduced-motion collapse, plus its arrival highlight and colour-only theme cross-fade.
- [3. Selection indicators](?path=/story/lab-product-ideas-3-selection-indicators--compare): the stock Tabs pill, line and RadioGroup against Perkcord's sliding 3px bar and weighted segmented control.
- [4. State and status colour](?path=/story/lab-product-ideas-4-state-and-status-colour--compare): the stock grey hover and single destructive Alert against Loominist's brand-tint wash and drasil's status triads with a left-rule callout.
- [5. Focus ring](?path=/story/lab-product-ideas-5-focus-ring--compare): the stock soft ring against Loominist's solid 2px brand outline, drawn inside menus, scroll areas and tab bars as in Chronote and Perkcord.
- [6. Lists and rows](?path=/story/lab-product-ideas-6-lists-and-rows--compare): stock Cards and Table rows against drasil's one-surface ruled list and Chronote's hover-reveal secondary actions.
- [7. Type voice](?path=/story/lab-product-ideas-7-type-voice--compare): stock Tailwind sizes against Perkcord's role-named type scale and Loominist's eyebrow with a display heading.
- [8. Touch and feedback](?path=/story/lab-product-ideas-8-touch-and-feedback--compare): stock target sizes and dismissal against Perkcord's 44px coarse-pointer targets and dirty-dialog nudge, and VRDex's selection pop.

To pick, tell the agent which option to keep in each group, or stock. Picked options are then built into the tokens and theme styles, and the rest of this folder is deleted.
